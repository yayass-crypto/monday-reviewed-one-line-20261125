One line reviewing my own Monday — the whole entry is the text `main.py`
writes to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Monday review: I circled the parking lot for twenty minutes, and the only free space was beside the car that keyed mine last week.

## Bounty

[Monday, Reviewed in One Line — make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-monday-reviewed-in-one-line-e55de3c1)

Write the funniest one-line review of your own Monday, under 200
characters, single line, no title and no explanation. A judge model
compares the lines two at a time against the published judge brief.
Prize $2 (1st $1, 2nd $0.40, 3rd $0.30, 4th $0.20, 5th $0.10), free entry.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
